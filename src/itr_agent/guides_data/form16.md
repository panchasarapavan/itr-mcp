# Form 16 (one per employer)
File type: PDF
1. Ask your employer's HR or payroll team for Form 16 (Part A and Part B) for the financial year
2. Part A can also be downloaded from TRACES (https://www.tdscpc.gov.in) under Downloads, once the employer has filed its TDS return
3. Save each employer's Form 16 separately
Note: Part B comes only from the employer. If you changed jobs in the year, you need one Form 16 per employer.
